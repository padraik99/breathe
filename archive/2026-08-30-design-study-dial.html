A 1:2 ratio, still near resonance. Vagal outflow to the heart rises during exhalation and is suppressed during inhalation, so weighting the breath toward the out-breath biases the whole cycle parasympathetic.
Presleep slow breathing has been shown to deepen slow-wave sleep across the night, though it did not shorten how long people took to fall asleep.
