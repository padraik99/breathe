Cue Audition

The transition cue, un-pitched. A tone has to find a hole in the harmony and a level that works over a dense bed and over silence; those fight each other, which is why every version so far has failed one or the other. Noise only has the level problem — and that one is solvable, because the level can be measured off the bed instead of guessed.
